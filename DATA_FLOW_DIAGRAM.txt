5. KEY FORMULAS REFERENCE
================================================================================

Inventory Updates:
  Raw = Previous + Purchased - Used_in_Production
  WIP = Previous + Produced - Assembled
  FG  = Previous + Assembled - Sold

Sales:
  Demand = f(Price, Marketing, Competition)
  Actual_Sales = min(Demand, Available_FG)
  Revenue = Actual_Sales × Price

COGS (Cost of Goods Sold):
  Unit_Cost = Material_per_unit + Production_per_unit + Assembly_per_unit
  COGS = Units_Sold × Unit_Cost

GuV (Income Statement):
  Gross_Profit = Revenue - COGS
  EBIT = Gross_Profit - Overhead - Marketing - Depreciation
  PBT = EBIT - Interest
  Tax = max(0, PBT × 0.3333)
  Net_Profit = PBT - Tax

Cash Flow:
  Cash_End = Cash_Begin + Receivables_Collected - Cash_Costs
  where Cash_Costs = Material + Production + Assembly +
                     Overhead + Marketing + Interest + Tax
                     (excludes Depreciation)

Receivables (1-Quarter Lag):
  Next_Quarter_Receivables = This_Quarter_Revenue
  This_Quarter_Cash_In = Previous_Quarter_Receivables
